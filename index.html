About Us

Makhozee Tahafuz Movement (MTM) is a community welfare organization working to help needy people across the shangla.

Our organization works together for the betterment of the area and helps in developing necessary facilities for the community.

MTM has no single specific person; it is a team effort where members work together for humanity and social welfare.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve humanity by helping needy families, supporting education, promoting community welfare, and working together for the development and prosperity of our society.

Our Vision

To build a united, educated, and prosperous community where every individual has access to basic facilities, equal opportunities, and a better quality of life.
